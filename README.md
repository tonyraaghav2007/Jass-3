# Jass ❤️ — I Love You Typographic Portrait

A romantic web application that recreates the portrait of **Jass** entirely out of repeating typographic text: **"I Love You ♥"**.

Crafted and optimized specifically for **Apple iPhone 12** (Retina @3x resolution, mobile gesture handling, safe-area notches, and PWA capabilities).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Typographic Recreation Engine**:
   - The entire image is reconstructed with precision-rendered typography: *"I Love You ♥"*.
   - Uses staggered sentence alignment and contrast preservation to ensure facial contours, eyes, lips, and hair strands are vivid and unmistakably clear.

2. **Interactive 4x Loupe (Magnifying Glass)**:
   - Touch and drag anywhere on the portrait (or hover on desktop) to bring up a crystal-glass magnifying lens.
   - Magnifies 4x so you can clearly read the individual words and letters shaping each feature.

3. **Split Compare Mode & Peek**:
   - Tap the split button or hold "Peek Original" to swipe between the original photo and the text recreation.

4. **iPhone 12 Optimization**:
   - Designed for the iPhone 12 screen aspect ratio (390px × 844px CSS resolution).
   - Dynamic viewport height (`100dvh`) and safe-area insets (`env(safe-area-inset-top)` and `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`).
   - Sleek iOS glassmorphic bottom control sheet.
   - PWA-ready: Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** in Safari for an edge-to-edge native full-screen experience.

5. **5 Romantic Color Themes**:
   - 🌸 **Vivid Natural**: Real skin tones, eyes, lips, and red dress.
   - 🌹 **Rose & Ruby**: Romantic blush pink, ruby, and champagne.
   - 🕯️ **Amber Gold**: Warm candlelight tones.
   - 💜 **Neon Amour**: Electric magenta, pink, and violet.
   - 🤍 **Noir Silver**: Classic monochrome elegance.

6. **Interactive Customization**:
   - Change the repeating message to any custom text (e.g. *"I Love You Jass"*).
   - Text density controls: **Ultra Detail (micro)**, **Balanced**, **Bold Graphic**.
   - Adjustable background underlay / glow blend (0% pure text to 80% rich glow).
   - Rotation control: Instant 0° / 90° / 180° / 270° orientation toggle.

7. **Ambient Romantic Music & Effects**:
   - Synthesizer playing soft romantic ambient piano chords and chimes (built with Web Audio API — 100% self-contained).
   - Floating romantic bokeh hearts and gentle star shimmer.

8. **One-Click 4K Download**:
   - Save high-resolution PNG artwork directly to camera roll or desktop.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

### 1. View on Computer:
Open your browser and navigate to:
```
http://localhost:8080
```

### 2. View on your iPhone 12:
Make sure your iPhone 12 is connected to the same Wi-Fi network, open **Safari**, and navigate to:
```
http://192.168.31.135:8080
```

> **Pro-Tip for iPhone 12:** Tap the **Share icon** at the bottom of Safari, then tap **"Add to Home Screen"**. It will launch like a full-screen native iOS app with no browser address bars!
